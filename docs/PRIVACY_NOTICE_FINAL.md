# AgoraX privacy notice

Version 1.2 · 22 November 2026

AgoraX is a student pilot: a test of whether students in Groningen want events that only go ahead when enough people sign up. Nothing is paid during the pilot. This notice explains what personal data the pilot uses, why, and what you can do about it.

## 2. What we keep

You never have to give your name, email or phone number to browse or to say you are interested in an event.

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Just by visiting | Nothing is stored on your device and no ID is created. We only add one to an anonymous visit count for the poster or shared link that brought you here, and for the version of the page you were shown. |
| From your first action (for example tapping "Interested") | A random ID for this browser, created by the anonymous sign-in (there are no accounts or passwords), when you first and last used the pilot, which poster or shared link brought you here, and which of the two versions of the page you were shown. |
| When you use the pilot | The events you are interested in, your answers to price questions, ideas you propose (and any picture you add), offers to help run an event, offers of a space, and suggestions. |
| Only if you fill it in | Your WhatsApp number and/or email, your first name, and which messages you agreed to, with the date and the exact wording you agreed to. |
| Only if you apply to organise | What you wrote about the events you would like to run, and our decision (approved or declined, and when). |
| Only if you add photos or videos to an event | The files. They are visible to every visitor of that event. Photos are made smaller on your device before upload, which removes camera details such as location. |
| Only if you allow statistics | Which event cards you saw and opened, and how far you scrolled. No name or contact details. Nothing is counted until you tap "Allow". |

We do not ask for sensitive data (such as health, religion or political views) and we ask you not to put it in any form.

## 3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

| What we do | Legal basis (GDPR article 6) |
|---|---|
| Counting visits per poster or link, without any ID | Legitimate interest (6(1)(f)): knowing which posters and links work. No data about you is stored. |
| Counting which events students are interested in, linked to the random browser ID, to decide which events to run | Legitimate interest (6(1)(f)): testing demand for the pilot. The data is pseudonymous and low-impact, and you can remove it at any time. |
| Comparing two versions of the page, to see which one helps students find events they would go to, using the version saved with the random browser ID | Legitimate interest (6(1)(f)): improving the pilot. The data is pseudonymous and low-impact, and is deleted with the rest of your pilot data. |
| Telling you whether an event you picked goes ahead, is cancelled or won't happen | Your consent (6(1)(a)), given with the tick box. |
| Talking to you about an event you offered to run, an idea you proposed, or a space you offered | Your consent (6(1)(a)), for that purpose only. |
| Handling your application to become an organiser | Legitimate interest (6(1)(f)): deciding who can run events in the pilot. |
| Visit statistics | Your consent (6(1)(a)), asked once. Before you say yes, nothing is counted. You can change your answer at any time under Privacy & my data (link at the bottom of every page). |
| Showing event photos and videos | Legitimate interest (6(1)(f)): showing what an event is like. Whoever uploads a photo confirms that the people in it agree. **If you appear in a photo or video and want it removed, email us and we remove it within 7 days.** |
| Keeping the site secure and preventing abuse | Legitimate interest (6(1)(f)). |

You can withdraw any consent at any time. This does not affect what was done before. You can object to anything based on legitimate interest by emailing us; we will stop unless we have a compelling reason, which for this pilot is unlikely.

We never sell your data, never use your number for advertising, and make no automated decisions about you.

## 5. Cookies and browser storage

AgoraX sets no cookies of its own, and the site's hosting sets none either. It keeps a few small items in your browser's storage: the anonymous sign-in, which poster or link brought you here, your settings, whether you closed a notice, your answer to the statistics question, which version of the page you were shown (from your first action, so it stays the same), and (only after you allow statistics) which cards were already counted. The Cookie Policy lists each item. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask to **see, correct, export or delete** your data, **object** to how we use it, or ask us to **limit** it. You can **withdraw consent** at any time.

- **Do it yourself:** Privacy & my data → Delete my contact details, or Delete everything.
- **Or email** mikhailalenikau@gmail.com. We answer within one month. To protect you, we may ask you to send the request from the browser or number it concerns.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the **Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens** (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl). We'd appreciate the chance to fix it first.

## 8. Age

The pilot is for people aged **16 and over**. Every form that asks for an email or number asks you to confirm you are 16 or older. If we learn that someone younger left contact details, we delete them.

## 9. Security and data leaks

Only the pilot's admins can read contact details. The database rules are tested, and the connection is encrypted. If a leak happens that could put you at risk, we report it to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens within 72 hours and tell you directly if the risk is high.

## 10. Changes

When this notice changes, we update the version and date at the top and say so in the app. If a change affects something you consented to, we ask you again.

The GDPR (in Dutch, AVG) and the Dutch implementation act (UAVG) apply.
